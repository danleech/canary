What this means for you: Rising prices squeeze real incomes, especially for households with large shares of spending on energy, food and rent. Higher inflation tends to keep US interest rates and borrowing costs elevated, which feeds through to mortgages, loans and global markets.

Why it matters: Inflation sets Fed policy and thus global borrowing costs; a resurgence would keep rates high for everyone.
