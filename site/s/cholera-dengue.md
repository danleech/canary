WHO counts over 3,000 cholera deaths in 2026, nearly nine in ten in Africa; DR Congo led new deaths in October

Why it's at this level: WHO said on 10 Nov that more than 3,000 people have died of cholera worldwide in 2026, nearly nine in ten in Africa, though deaths in the first nine months were lower than in 2025 (AFP via The Standard). ECDC's 27 Oct update counts 501 new deaths since 28 Sep, led by DR Congo with 288, Nigeria with 114 and Cameroon with 67. WHO reported a surge in Yemen as conflict intensified (Al Jazeera, 13 Oct). High mortality in conflict-affected countries keeps the Alert.

What this means for you: Risk is concentrated in conflict and displacement settings with poor water and sanitation. Travellers to affected areas can check NaTHNaC TravelHealthPro and FCDO travel advice for country-specific guidance.

What moves it: Conflict, floods, heat, El Niño, water and sanitation, mosquito range expansion.
