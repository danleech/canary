DR Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,504 confirmed cases and 5,630 deaths by 16 Nov; transmission continues

Why it's at this level: ECDC's 19 Nov update reports 11,504 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases and 5,630 deaths in DR Congo to 16 Nov across eight provinces, with community transmission ongoing (ECDC). WHO's 6 Nov bulletin listed Kenya at 9 cases and an imported case in Nigeria, with no new country since 23 Oct (WHO). Africa CDC said on 15 Nov the outbreak is not under control (Al Jazeera). Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Travellers to DR Congo and neighbouring countries should follow FCDO travel advice and WHO guidance. Anyone returning from affected areas with fever should call NHS 111 before attending in person.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
