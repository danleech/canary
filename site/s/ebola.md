Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak in DR Congo reached 11,504 confirmed cases and 5,630 deaths by 16 Nov, per ECDC

Why it's at this level: ECDC's 19 Nov update counts 11,504 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases and 5,630 related deaths in DR Congo on data to 16 Nov, with community transmission ongoing in eight provinces (ECDC). UKHSA's report for the week to 15 Nov describes it as the largest recorded Bundibugyo outbreak, with 68 health zones affected, Ituri and North Kivu worst hit, and contact follow-up below target (UKHSA). Kenya has had no new case since 19 Oct and Nigeria's single imported case on 20 Oct has had no follow-on cases (UKHSA). Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Risk to the UK is low, per UKHSA. Anyone travelling to DR Congo or neighbouring countries should check FCDO travel advice, and anyone unwell after returning should call NHS 111 and mention their travel.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
