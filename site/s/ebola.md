DR Congo Ebola outbreak reaches 10,668 confirmed cases and 5,198 deaths; 1,463 new cases in 21 days

Why it's at this level: WHO reported 10,668 confirmed cases and 5,198 deaths in DR Congo as of 3 Nov, with 1,463 new cases in the previous 21 days and active transmission in 51 health zones across eight provinces (WHO, 6 Nov). Kenya and Nigeria have ongoing cases, and a medically evacuated patient in the UK was reported on 5 Nov (WHO). ECDC counts Kenya and Nigeria as newly affected in October.

What this means for you: Follow NHS and NaTHNaC advice if travelling to DR Congo or neighbouring countries; UKHSA rates the UK risk as very low.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
