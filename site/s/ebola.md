DR Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,197 confirmed cases and 5,471 deaths by 11 Nov; Kenya and Nigeria hit in October

Why it's at this level: ECDC's update of 13 Nov reports 11,197 confirmed cases and 5,471 deaths in DR Congo, with data to 11 Nov and community transmission ongoing (ECDC). UKHSA's report for the week to 8 Nov puts the national average at 65–67 new cases a day, with North Kivu now ahead of Ituri. Kenya (nine cases, two deaths) and Nigeria (one case, 20 Oct) were affected in October; Uganda's outbreak ended on 27 Aug. Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Travellers to DR Congo and neighbouring countries should follow FCDO travel advice and UKHSA guidance. UKHSA rates the risk to the UK population as low; WHO rates it very high in DR Congo.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.
