DR Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,200 cases and 5,500 deaths; Africa CDC says it is still not under control

Why it's at this level: Africa CDC's director-general said on 15 Nov that government data show more than 11,200 infections and 5,500 deaths, that over 80% of new cases come from outside contact lists and over 65% of deaths occur in the community (Gulf Today, 17 Nov; Al Jazeera). About $2.9bn has been pledged and he urged donors to release $1.6bn. Conflict is hampering the response (Africanews, 12 Nov). Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Follow FCDO travel advice and NaTHNaC guidance for DR Congo. The UKHSA rates the risk to the UK public as very low.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.
