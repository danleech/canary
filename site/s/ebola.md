DR Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases and 5,387 deaths by 11 Nov; WHO says new hotspots keep emerging

Why it's at this level: DR Congo's Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases with 5,387 deaths by 11 Nov, the country's deadliest on record (Al Jazeera, 11 Nov). ECDC had counted 10,920 confirmed cases and 5,323 deaths to 7 Nov across eight provinces (ECDC, 9 Nov). WHO's Director-General said on 11 Nov there are signs of slowing in some areas but new hotspots continue to emerge. Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Risk outside affected areas remains low. Travellers should check FCDO travel advice for DR Congo and follow WHO and UKHSA guidance.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
