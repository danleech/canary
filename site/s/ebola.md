DR Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,197 confirmed cases and 5,471 deaths by 11 Nov; Kenya and Nigeria hit in October

Why it's at this level: ECDC's update of 13 Nov reports 11,197 confirmed cases and 5,471 deaths in DR Congo, with data to 11 Nov and community transmission ongoing in eight provinces (ECDC). Congolese government data cited on 11 Nov put cases above 11,000, the country's largest outbreak on record (Al Jazeera). Kenya (nine cases, two deaths) and Nigeria (one imported case) reported cases in October; Uganda's outbreak has ended. Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Risk is concentrated in eastern and central DR Congo. People travelling to the region, health workers and aid staff should follow FCDO travel advice, UKHSA guidance and WHO advice; returning travellers with fever should seek NHS advice.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
