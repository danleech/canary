DR Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,376 confirmed cases and 5,568 deaths by 15 Nov; Africa CDC says not under control

Why it's at this level: ECDC reports 11,376 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases and 5,568 deaths in DR Congo as of 15 Nov across eight provinces, with Kenya at 9 cases and a case in Nigeria reported on 20 Oct; Uganda's outbreak has ended (ECDC). Africa CDC said on 15 Nov the outbreak is still not under control (Al Jazeera). Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Risk to travellers outside affected areas is very low. Anyone travelling to DR Congo or neighbouring countries should follow FCDO travel advice and UKHSA guidance, and seek medical advice if unwell after return.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.
