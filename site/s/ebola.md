DR Congo Ebola reached 10,920 confirmed cases and 5,323 deaths by 7 Nov per ECDC; outbreak spans eight provinces

Why it's at this level: ECDC reported on 9 Nov that DR Congo had 10,920 confirmed cases and 5,323 deaths to 7 Nov, up 179 cases since 6 Nov, across eight provinces (ECDC). On 11 Nov WHO's Director-General cited signs of slowing in some areas but said new hotspots continue to emerge and insecurity is hampering the response (WHO). Kenya has nine cases and two deaths, none new since 20 Oct, and Nigeria's case of 20 Oct was evacuated to Switzerland. Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Risk outside DR Congo and its neighbours is rated low by WHO. Travellers to the region can check FCDO travel advice; anyone unwell after returning can follow NHS and UKHSA guidance on seeking care.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
