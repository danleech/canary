Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak reached 11,145 confirmed cases and 5,437 deaths by 10 Nov, mostly in DR Congo

Why it's at this level: CDC data to 10 Nov count 11,145 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases and 5,437 deaths, 11,114 cases in DR Congo, with cases also in Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria and France (Contagion Live). ECDC's October update showed eight DR Congo provinces affected (ECDC). Africa CDC said on 15 Nov the outbreak is not under control (Al Jazeera). Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Follow FCDO travel advice and UKHSA guidance for DR Congo and neighbouring countries. The risk to the UK public remains very low per UKHSA.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
