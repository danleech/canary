DR Congo Ebola outbreak passes 10,500 confirmed cases and 5,100 deaths; cases also in Kenya and Nigeria

Why it's at this level: ECDC counted 10,532 confirmed cases and 5,126 deaths in DR Congo as of 2 Nov, with 65 new cases reported that day and eight provinces affected; Kenya has nine confirmed cases and Nigeria reported one on 20 Oct (ECDC). Since 3 Nov, travellers to the US must attest they have not been in DR Congo in the previous 21 days (Nature World News).

What this means for you: Follow NaTHNaC and UKHSA advice if travelling to DR Congo, Kenya or neighbouring countries; ECDC rates the risk to people in Europe as very low.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
