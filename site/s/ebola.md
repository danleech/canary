DR Congo Ebola outbreak at 10,741 confirmed cases and 5,230 deaths on 5 Nov; Nigeria's first case came on 20 Oct

Why it's at this level: ECDC reported on 6 Nov that DR Congo had 10,741 confirmed cases and 5,230 related deaths as of 5 Nov, with community transmission across eight provinces; WHO counted 10,668 cases as of 3 Nov. WHO's Director-General said on 4 Nov that transmission is slowing in some areas but new hotspots keep emerging. Kenya has nine cases, including two deaths, the last reported on 20 Oct, and Nigeria reported one case on 20 Oct (ECDC). No newer official count was found as of 9 Nov.

What this means for you: WHO rates the risk as very high in DR Congo, high in neighbouring countries and low globally. Travellers can follow FCDO travel advice and NaTHNaC; UKHSA and NHS guidance applies to anyone unwell after returning from an affected area.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
