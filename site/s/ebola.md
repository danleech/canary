DR Congo Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak reaches 12,021 confirmed cases and 5,904 deaths by 24 Nov, ECDC says

Why it's at this level: ECDC's 25 Nov update counts 12,021 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases and 5,904 related deaths in DR Congo on data to 24 Nov, up from 11,504 cases a week earlier, with eight provinces affected and community transmission ongoing (ECDC). Africa CDC said on 15 Nov that the outbreak is still not under control (Al Jazeera). Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: UKHSA and NHS advise anyone returning from affected areas with fever to call NHS 111 or seek urgent care. Follow FCDO travel advice for DR Congo.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
