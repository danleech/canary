DR Congo Ebola outbreak at 10,741 confirmed cases and 5,230 deaths on 5 Nov; spread reached a province bordering CAR

Why it's at this level: ECDC reported on 6 Nov that DR Congo had 10,741 confirmed cases and 5,230 related deaths as of 5 Nov, across eight provinces. UKHSA's week 44 report counts 472 more cases in the week to 1 Nov and lists Yakoma, on the Central African Republic border, as the latest zone affected. Kenya has nine cases, none new since 19 Oct, and Nigeria's single case of 20 Oct has produced no further cases among 62 contacts. Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Risk to the UK is assessed as low by UKHSA. Anyone travelling to DR Congo or neighbouring countries should follow FCDO travel advice, and anyone unwell after return should call NHS 111 and mention their travel.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
