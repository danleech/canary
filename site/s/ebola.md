DR Congo Ebola outbreak reaches 10,741 confirmed cases and 5,230 deaths; Kenya and Nigeria affected in October

Why it's at this level: ECDC reported on 6 Nov that DR Congo had 10,741 confirmed cases and 5,230 related deaths as of 5 Nov, with community transmission ongoing across eight provinces. Kenya confirmed its first case on 6 Oct and had nine cases, including two deaths, by 26 Oct; Nigeria reported a case on 20 Oct (ECDC).

What this means for you: The risk is concentrated in affected areas of DR Congo and neighbouring countries. Travellers can check FCDO travel advice and UKHSA guidance; anyone returning from affected areas with symptoms can follow NHS and UKHSA advice.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
