DR Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,197 confirmed cases and 5,471 deaths by 11 Nov, ECDC says

Why it's at this level: ECDC's update of 13 Nov reports 11,197 confirmed cases and 5,471 deaths in DR Congo, with data to 11 Nov and community transmission in eight provinces; Kenya has nine cases and two deaths, with its last case on 20 Oct, and Uganda's outbreak has ended (ECDC). Cases passed 11,000 by 11 Nov (Al Jazeera). Nigeria reported an imported case on 20 Oct. Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Risk outside affected areas remains low. Travellers to DR Congo and neighbouring countries should follow FCDO travel advice and WHO guidance; anyone unwell after returning should call NHS 111 and mention their travel.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.
