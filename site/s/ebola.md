Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak in DR Congo reached 11,504 confirmed cases and 5,630 deaths by 16 Nov, per ECDC

Why it's at this level: ECDC's 19 Nov update counts 11,504 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases and 5,630 related deaths in DR Congo on data to 16 Nov, up 128 cases and 64 deaths since its previous update (ECDC). UKHSA reports 68 health zones affected across eight provinces, with Ituri and North Kivu worst hit; Kenya has had no new case since 19 Oct and Uganda's outbreak ended on 27 Aug (UKHSA). Africa CDC said on 15 Nov the outbreak was still not under control (Al Jazeera). Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: UKHSA assesses the risk to the UK as low. Anyone travelling to DR Congo or neighbouring countries should follow FCDO travel advice and seek medical help promptly if feverish within 21 days of return.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
