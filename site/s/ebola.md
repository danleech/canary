Why it's at this level: ECDC reported on 13 Nov 11,197 confirmed cases and 5,471 deaths in DR Congo to 11 Nov, with community transmission in eight provinces; Kenya has nine cases and Uganda's outbreak has ended (ECDC). CDC's 15 Nov update counts 11,285 confirmed cases and 5,509 deaths across five countries to 12 Nov (CDC). WHO's Africa office said on 15 Nov that 400–500 new cases are reported weekly (WHO). Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Risk outside the affected region remains low, according to WHO. Travellers to DR Congo, Uganda or Kenya should follow FCDO travel advice and NHS fit for travel guidance; anyone unwell after return should call NHS 111 before attending.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.
