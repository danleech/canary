Bundibugyo Ebola passes 10,000 confirmed cases in DR Congo; imported cases in Kenya and Nigeria

Why it's at this level: ECDC counted 10,274 confirmed cases and 4,992 deaths in DR Congo to 28 Oct, across eight provinces (ECDC). WHO's 23 Oct update reported nine cases in Kenya, including one death, since the first import on 6 Oct, and an imported case in Abuja, Nigeria, on 20 Oct, the fifth country affected (WHO). Africa CDC's wider count, including suspected cases, is higher.

What this means for you: Follow UKHSA, WHO and FCDO travel health advice for DR Congo, Uganda, Kenya and Nigeria. The risk to people in the UK remains very low.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
