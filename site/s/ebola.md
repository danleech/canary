WHO counts 11,613 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases and 5,678 deaths in DR Congo to 17 Nov; Kenya and Nigeria hit

Why it's at this level: WHO's 20 Nov outbreak news counts 11,613 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases including 5,678 deaths in DR Congo to 17 Nov, across 68 health zones in eight provinces, 48 still active; 452 of 720 deaths since 28 Oct occurred in communities (WHO). ECDC reported nine cases in Kenya and one in Nigeria (ECDC, 19 Nov), and its later update puts confirmed DR Congo cases above 12,000. Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Follow WHO and UKHSA guidance and FCDO travel advice for DR Congo, Kenya and Nigeria. The risk to people in the UK and EU is rated very low.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
