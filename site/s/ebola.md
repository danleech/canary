DR Congo Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak at 11,504 confirmed cases and 5,630 deaths by 16 Nov; 400–500 new cases a week

Why it's at this level: ECDC's 19 Nov update counts 11,504 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases and 5,630 related deaths in DR Congo on data to 16 Nov, with Ituri province accounting for 7,911 cases (ECDC). WHO said on 17 Nov that 400–500 new cases are reported each week across 67 health zones in eight provinces, two-thirds of deaths occur outside treatment centres, and Kenya has reported nine cases and Nigeria one imported case (WHO). Cases far exceed the 1,000 Alert line.

What this means for you: Risk in the UK is very low. Follow UKHSA and NHS advice if you have returned from DR Congo, and FCDO travel advice before going.

Why it matters: Ebola, Marburg and similar viruses kill a large share of those infected; outbreaks that reach cities or cross borders can overwhelm health systems.
What moves it: Surveillance and contact tracing, vaccine availability (none licensed for some strains), conflict, cross-border travel, health-worker infections.

Rubric — Alert: More than 1,000 cases, or spread to a new country in the past 30 days. | Watch: An active outbreak contained within one country, or fewer than 1,000 cases. | Normal: No active Ebola/Marburg outbreak, or outbreaks declining with no new countries affected in 60 days.
