Why it's at this level: Client redemptions from nontraded BDCs reached $12.7bn in the first half of 2026, and Blackstone's BCRED received second-quarter requests of about 10% of shares, double its usual 5% limit (InvestmentNews, 18 Nov). Searches on 28 Nov found no simultaneous gating by several large funds, no forced asset sales and no losses spreading to banks or insurers, so Watch holds.

What this means for you: Holders of semi-liquid private credit funds and nontraded BDCs may have only part of a withdrawal request paid in a quarter while requests exceed caps. Bank depositors and insurance policyholders are not directly exposed on current evidence.

Why it matters: Private credit has grown fast outside bank regulation; its losses surface late and can spill into banks, insurers and pension funds.
What moves it: Defaults among leveraged mid-sized borrowers, payment-in-kind interest, fund redemption requests, bank lending to funds.

Rubric — Alert: Several large funds gate at once, forced asset sales, or losses spreading to banks or insurers. | Watch: A large fund limits redemptions, listed BDCs trade at wide discounts to NAV, or a cluster of borrower defaults with heavy losses. | Normal: No notable fund gates, defaults or markdowns beyond the routine.
