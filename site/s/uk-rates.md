What this means for you: Rising prices squeeze household budgets, especially for drivers and heating. Borrowers on variable or expiring fixed-rate mortgages are most exposed to further Bank Rate rises.

Why it matters: UK inflation and the Bank Rate set mortgage, savings and business borrowing costs for the whole country.
