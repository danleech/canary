JNIM insurgency persists in Mali; its declared Bamako blockade was reported as incomplete in September

Why it's at this level

JNIM declared a total blockade of Bamako in spring 2026, but a report of 25 Sep described it as incomplete, with fuel convoys reaching the city under army escort (Ivorian.net). A checkpoint on Bamako's outskirts was attacked on 18–19 Sep (Le Matin d'Algérie), and Human Rights Watch documented abuses by all sides on 19 Oct. Reporting was quiet as of 8 Nov: searches found no fall of a capital or major city and no spread of mass-casualty attacks to coastal states, though coverage was thin.

What this means for you

Civilians in central and northern Mali and in Burkina Faso are most exposed, along with fuel supply on the roads into Bamako. FCDO travel advice is the official reference for the region. Convoy attacks tend to bring fuel shortages and higher prices.
