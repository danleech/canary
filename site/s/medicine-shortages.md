Chemotherapy drug shortages persist after a Pfizer plant flood

Why it's at this level: ASHP added cytarabine (7 Oct) and irinotecan (12 Oct) injections to its US shortage list, linking part of the rise to flooding at a Pfizer plant in India, a disruption Pfizer expects to last into 2028 (Becker's, 26 Oct). In a September survey every cancer centre reported a shortage and 7% said they could not continue some treatment.

What this means for you: Patients should follow advice from their clinician or pharmacist; the NHS manages UK shortages through Serious Shortage Protocols.

Why it matters: Shipping disruption, factory failures and export curbs can leave essential medicines short, as with US IV fluids after Hurricane Helene in 2024.
What moves it: Manufacturing concentrated in India and China, shipping, raw materials, demand spikes, regulation.
