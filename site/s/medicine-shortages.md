Why it's at this level: NHSBSA's list checked on 19 Nov shows seven active Serious Shortage Protocols, for lansoprazole orodispersible tablets to 15 Jan 2027 and Estradot patches and Creon 25000 capsules to 8 Jan 2027, with none issued since September (NHSBSA). On 17 Nov the National Pharmacy Association estimated shortages could affect up to 6.4 million prescription items a month in 2026; price concessions hit a record 254 in July and 245 in October (Pharmaceutical Journal). No widespread rationing across major health systems was found, so Watch holds.

What this means for you: Patients on affected medicines may be given an alternative strength or form by their pharmacist under an SSP. The NHS and NHSBSA publish details; anyone unable to get a prescribed medicine should speak to their pharmacist or GP.

Why it matters: Shipping disruption, factory failures and export curbs can leave essential medicines short, as with US IV fluids after Hurricane Helene in 2024.
What moves it: Manufacturing concentrated in India and China, shipping, raw materials, demand spikes, regulation.
