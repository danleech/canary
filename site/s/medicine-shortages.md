Why it's at this level: NHSBSA's list checked on 17 Nov shows active Serious Shortage Protocols for lansoprazole orodispersible tablets to 15 Jan 2027, and for Estradot patches and Creon 25000 capsules to 8 Jan 2027, with none issued in November (NHSBSA). A Pfizer joint-venture plant in India closed by floods in July may not resume supply until 2028, prolonging shortages of cytarabine, irinotecan and bleomycin (CancerNetwork, 2 Nov). No widespread rationing was found.

What this means for you: If a usual medicine is unavailable, a UK pharmacist may supply an alternative under a Serious Shortage Protocol. NHS guidance advises not to stop a medicine without speaking to a pharmacist or GP.

Why it matters: Shipping disruption, factory failures and export curbs can leave essential medicines short, as with US IV fluids after Hurricane Helene in 2024.
What moves it: Manufacturing concentrated in India and China, shipping, raw materials, demand spikes, regulation.
