UK Serious Shortage Protocols cover lansoprazole, Estradot and Creon into January; no widespread rationing found

Why it's at this level: NHSBSA lists active Serious Shortage Protocols for lansoprazole orodispersible tablets from 9 Sep to 15 Jan 2027, and for Estradot patches and Creon 25000 capsules to 8 Jan 2027 after amendments on 2 Oct. Lansoprazole orodispersible supply is not expected to recover until early January, and pharmacists may limit supply to one month (Nursing in Practice). Searches to 10 Nov found no widespread rationing or cancelled treatment across major health systems.

What this means for you: Some UK patients may be given a different strength or form of these medicines under the protocols. The NHS advises speaking to a pharmacist or GP before changing or stopping a treatment.

Why it matters: Shipping disruption, factory failures and export curbs can leave essential medicines short, as with US IV fluids after Hurricane Helene in 2024.
What moves it: Manufacturing concentrated in India and China, shipping, raw materials, demand spikes, regulation.
