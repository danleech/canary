Why it's at this level: NHSBSA's list checked on 18 Nov shows seven active Serious Shortage Protocols: lansoprazole orodispersible tablets to 15 Jan 2027, and Estradot patches and Creon 25000 capsules to 8 Jan 2027, with none newer than September (NHSBSA). In the US, shortages of cytarabine, irinotecan, carboplatin and other chemotherapy drugs persist; a September survey found 7% of cancer centres could not continue some treatments (Becker's, 26 Oct). Searches on 18 Nov found no widespread rationing across major health systems, so Watch holds.

What this means for you: Under a Serious Shortage Protocol, pharmacists can supply an alternative without a new prescription. People affected can speak to their pharmacist or GP; NHS guidance covers how SSPs work.

Why it matters: Shipping disruption, factory failures and export curbs can leave essential medicines short, as with US IV fluids after Hurricane Helene in 2024.
What moves it: Manufacturing concentrated in India and China, shipping, raw materials, demand spikes, regulation.
