Why it's at this level: On 17 Nov the National Pharmacy Association estimated that shortages could have affected up to 6.4 million prescription items a month in 2026, based on 14 government supply notifications, and urged government action; price concessions reached a record 254 in July (Pharmaceutical Journal). What this means for you: Some patients may need a substitute or a return visit to the pharmacy. The NHS advises ordering repeat prescriptions in good time and speaking to your pharmacist or GP if a medicine is unavailable.

Why it matters: Shipping disruption, factory failures and export curbs can leave essential medicines short, as with US IV fluids after Hurricane Helene in 2024.
What moves it: Manufacturing concentrated in India and China, shipping, raw materials, demand spikes, regulation.
