Shortages span several countries, but searches on 23 Nov found no widespread rationing across major health systems, so Watch holds.

What this means for you: If a pharmacy cannot supply a medicine, ask the pharmacist or GP about alternatives; do not stop treatment without advice. NHS and pharmacy guidance covers what to do.

Why it matters: Shipping disruption, factory failures and export curbs can leave essential medicines short, as with US IV fluids after Hurricane Helene in 2024.
What moves it: Manufacturing concentrated in India and China, shipping, raw materials, demand spikes, regulation.
