FEWS NET assesses risk of Famine in Sudan, South Sudan and Somalia; no area classified as Famine as of 11 Nov

Why it's at this level: FEWS NET's East Africa outlook to January 2027 assesses a credible risk of Famine in parts of North Darfur and around Kadugli and Dilling in Sudan, four counties of Jonglei and Upper Nile in South Sudan, and agropastoral and riverine areas of Somalia. It says Famine would occur if conflict or sieges cut populations off from food, or in Somalia after a third poor harvest. No area is classified as Famine (IPC Phase 5) as of 11 Nov.

What this means for you: Risk of famine tends to bring urgent UN appeals and displacement towards neighbouring countries. WFP, UNICEF and the IPC publish the official assessments.

Why it matters: Famine is the most extreme humanitarian failure, almost always man-made by conflict.
What moves it: War, sieges, aid access, harvest failure, funding cuts.
