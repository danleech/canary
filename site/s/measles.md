Loss of elimination meets Watch; deaths are not clearly rising across countries, so the Alert line is not crossed.

What this means for you: Measles spreads very easily. Check that you and your children have had two MMR doses, and follow NHS and UKHSA advice, including before travel to North America.

Why it matters: Falling vaccination rates are bringing back diseases once eliminated; measles is the canary for the rest.
What moves it: Vaccine coverage, misinformation, health-system disruption.
