PAHO has concluded the US has lost measles elimination status, US media reported 14 Nov; 10 US deaths in 2026

Why it's at this level: PAHO concluded during the week of 9 Nov that measles transmission has been re-established in the US, ending elimination status held since 2000, according to researchers with knowledge of its deliberations; a public announcement was planned for 17 Nov (KFF Health News, 14 Nov). The CDC counts over 5,400 US cases in 2026, more than double 2025, and states report 10 measles-associated deaths this year; PAHO had not commented publicly (Contagion Live, 14 Nov). Loss of elimination status meets Watch.

What this means for you: Two doses of MMR vaccine prevent about 97% of infections. Check your and your children's vaccination records against NHS or CDC guidance, and follow official travel health advice before visiting areas with outbreaks.

Why it matters: Falling vaccination rates are bringing back diseases once eliminated; measles is the canary for the rest.
What moves it: Vaccine coverage, misinformation, health-system disruption.
