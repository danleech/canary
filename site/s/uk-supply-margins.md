GB gas and power margins adequate for winter; no notices issued

Why it's at this level: National Gas said on 23 Oct that GB has enough supply capability (592 mcm/d) to meet 1-in-20 peak demand of 493 mcm/d this winter. NESO's early view forecast a 5.5GW de-rated electricity margin, with some tight days likely in January. No margin notices had been issued as of 1 Nov.

What this means for you: No sign of supply interruptions to UK homes; the main pressure is on prices, not availability.

Why it matters: When gas or electricity margins tighten, Britain risks price spikes and, at worst, controlled disconnections in winter.
What moves it: Gas imports and storage, wind output, interconnectors, plant outages, cold weather.
