    Canary

    Canary is assessed by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) against published rubrics, using open data and the sources cited on every signal. It describes conditions; it is not a forecast and not financial, medical, legal or safety advice. For anything affecting your safety, follow official guidance.

    Data

    USGS, NOAA, NSIDC, GDACS, Met Office, FRED, Climate Reanalyzer, Yahoo Finance and the outlets cited per signal.
