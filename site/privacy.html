In short

Canary doesn't ask who you are. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies, on the website or in the app. Canary publishes the same public board to everyone and keeps no personal information about the people who read it.

Two companies that run parts of the service see the network details any website or app request carries, such as your IP address: Cloudflare, which hosts Canary, and Apple, if you use the app or buy Canary+. The website also loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Each is described below.

The website

Hosting. canary.danleech.com, its data files, feed and the endpoint for AI assistants are served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and request details (the page, time, browser type) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, and browsers may send it reports of failed connections. Canary adds no analytics or tracking scripts. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Fonts. Pages load the IBM Plex typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it fetches them. See Google Fonts and privacy.

Your settings. The site remembers your theme and how you've arranged the board in your browser's local storage. That stays on your device, is never sent to Canary, and you can clear it in your browser's settings.

Links. Every signal links to its sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

The iPhone app

No account and no tracking. The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code. It doesn't use your location, contacts, photos or any other personal data on your iPhone.

Network requests. The app downloads the public board from canary.danleech.com, through Cloudflare as above. Requests identify themselves only as the Canary app and carry no device or advertising identifier.

On your device. The signals you pin, your alert settings and a saved copy of the board are stored on your iPhone and shared only with Canary's own widgets there. Deleting the app deletes them.

Alerts. If you turn on alerts, your iPhone checks the public board in the background and shows a notification when a signal you follow changes level. The notification is created on your iPhone; there is no Canary push server and no device token.

Canary+. Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. Canary never receives your name, Apple Account or payment details; the app only asks Apple, on your iPhone, whether a subscription is active. See Apple's privacy policy.

Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight and widgets run through iOS on your device and use only the public board.

What Canary never does

  • Sell or share personal information, or use it for advertising.
  • Track you across other apps or websites.
  • Build a profile of you, or use anything about you to train AI models. Canary's AI assessor reads public sources about world events, never information about readers.

Canary is for a general audience and isn't directed at children.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held about you. Canary holds none, so there is nothing for it to return or delete; information processed by Cloudflare, Google or Apple is covered by their notices above. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes and contact

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too, and the history is public in the project repository. Questions about privacy are welcome as an issue in the project repository.
